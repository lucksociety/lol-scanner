# Exclude women's competitions

Owner policy excludes women's sports. The existing game-level model uses the tier-one/open international corpus (Worlds, MSI, First Stand, and major regional leagues); no women's tournament was present in that production scope. Add explicit women's-competition guards to schedule ingestion/flattening, corpus tournament selection, market discovery and cached Discord cards. Named women's circuits (including Ignis Cup and Equal Esports) and explicit female/women metadata are excluded. This is competition-level policy, not an inference about individual players' genders. All model inputs remain market-free; scoring coefficients and historical audit files are unchanged.

Validation: 80 model tests, three Worker tests and 15 learning/evergreen tests passed. No Discord messages sent. Worker only dispatches main, so no Worker bundle update is required.
